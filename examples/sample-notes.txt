DEMONSTRATION NOTES — authored for testing this application, not quoted from a book.

This example describes a personal reflection routine. Write down one clear goal
in the morning. Spend two minutes imagining the actions you could take toward it.
Record three things you appreciate. Pick one small, concrete action for today.
In the evening, review the action and adjust tomorrow's plan.

In these demonstration notes, "vibration" is used as a metaphor for a person's
emotional state and attitude. The routine does not guarantee money or external events.
There are no numerical sound frequencies or medical treatments in these notes.
